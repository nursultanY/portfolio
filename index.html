Nursultan Yerken

2080-O

Education City, Al Luqta St, Ar Rayyan

Doha,Qatar

As a Mechanical Engineering student at Hamad Bin Khalifa University, I have been captivated by the dynamic realm where engineering, energy systems, and data-driven technologies intersect with real-world industrial impact. My enthusiasm for this convergence has guided me toward a journey of technical exploration and applied research, primarily focusing on mechanical systems design, energy optimization, and machine learning applications in engineering.

Throughout my academic tenure at Hamad Bin Khalifa University, I have built a strong foundation in Mechanical Engineering, expanding my perspective through rigorous coursework such as Statics, Thermodynamics, Measurements & Instrumentation, and Principles of Electrical Engineering. These courses have equipped me with both analytical depth and practical engineering intuition, enabling me to bridge theoretical knowledge with hands-on implementation.

During my internship at the Qatar Environment & Energy Research Institute (QEERI), I developed a Python-based data pipeline processing over 3,000 battery cycles, reducing analysis time by 60%. I benchmarked eight Machine Learning models for battery State of Health and State of Charge prediction, achieving an R² of 0.92 on unseen data. My contributions led to co-authoring two IEEE conference publications and validating robust data-splitting methodologies to eliminate data leakage. This experience strengthened my ability to integrate engineering principles with advanced analytics to solve real-world energy challenges.

In my role as a Shell Eco-Marathon Body-Chassis Unit Member at Texas A&M University at Qatar, I led the design and optimization of a high-strength tubular chassis using SOLIDWORKS, improving structural stiffness by 25% through Finite Element Analysis. I engineered hydraulic braking and steering systems and developed lightweight structural components that reduced mass while meeting stress requirements. This hands-on involvement deepened my expertise in mechanical design, structural validation, and performance optimization under real testing conditions.

As a Field Engineer Intern at NefteStroiService Ltd., I operated directly within active oilfield environments, conducting 150+ pressure and temperature diagnostics and resolving critical equipment non-conformities. This experience refined my technical discipline, operational awareness, and ability to coordinate multidisciplinary engineering teams under time-sensitive constraints.

My research experience as a Research Assistant under Dr. Hasan Kurban at HBKU further expanded my analytical capabilities. I spearheaded pulsar classification using a Kolmogorov-Arnold Network, achieving 98.5% accuracy and extracting symbolic formulas from complex datasets. By applying advanced feature engineering techniques such as Kernel Density Estimation and Permutation Entropy, I reduced data noise and improved predictive performance, reinforcing my commitment to combining computational intelligence with engineering applications.

In my project undertakings, including Invent for the Planet, I led the technical development of a modular biodegradable carbon-capture cell, reducing projected manufacturing costs from $150 to $10 per square meter. Utilizing SOLIDWORKS (CSWP-certified), Python, and simulation tools, I translated conceptual ideas into validated prototypes within accelerated development cycles.

My proficiency in engineering and computational tools — including SOLIDWORKS, Ansys Fluent, Python, and LaTeX — combined with certifications such as SOLIDWORKS CAD Design Professional and Complete Python Mastery, has shaped me into a multidisciplinary engineer capable of integrating mechanical systems, data science, and performance-driven innovation.

This journey has shaped my aspirations and solidified my commitment to advancing sustainable energy systems, intelligent engineering design, and data-enhanced decision-making — fueling my ambition to create measurable, real-world impact at the intersection of engineering and technology.
